# LorePin

Pin personal notes to locations on the World of Warcraft world map.

월드맵 위치에 개인 메모를 핀으로 고정합니다.

## Features

- **Personal Pins**: Drop pins at any location with your own title and note
- **Categories**: Organize pins by category (Quest, Danger, Gather, Scenic, Complete) with custom categories support
- **World Map**: View your pins on the world map
- **Account-wide**: All your characters share the same pin database
- **Search, Filter & Sort**: Search pins by title or note, filter by category, sort by date or map
- **Localization**: English and Korean supported

## Why LorePin?

Existing addons serve different purposes:
- **TomTom**: Waypoint navigation (deletes on arrival)
- **HandyNotes**: Community-maintained POI database (not editable)
- **LorePin**: Your personal notes, your discoveries, your way

## Installation

1. Download from [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/lorepin) (coming soon)
2. Extract to `World of Warcraft/_retail_/Interface/AddOns/`
3. Restart WoW or `/reload`

## Usage

- `/lp` — Open pin creation popup at current location
- `/lp <title>` — Open pin creation popup with the title filled in
- `/lp list` — Show all pins
- `/lp settings` — Open settings panel
- `/lp help` — Show all commands

## Requirements

- World of Warcraft: Midnight (12.0.0+)
- Interface: 120105
